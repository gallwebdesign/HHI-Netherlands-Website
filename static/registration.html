Before you register

The checklist.

  • Roster locked. All dancers confirmed, ages match your division.
  • Rulebook read. Routine length, music and props per the official regulations.
  • Music clean. No explicit lyrics; edit ready before the deadline.
  • Point of contact. One reachable crew manager for all communication.
