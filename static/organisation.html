Organisation

The crew behind the crews.

Hip Hop International Netherlands holds the official Dutch license of Hip Hop International and has produced the national championship since 2015.

One mission: Dutch crews on the world stage.

Hip Hop International, founded in Los Angeles in 2002, produces the World Hip Hop Dance Championship — the biggest street-dance competition on the planet, with crews from over fifty countries.

As the official license holder we bring that world stage to the Netherlands: one fair, professional championship where every crew gets the same shot at proving themselves.

What we do

Production

The event

Stage, lights, sound and a schedule that runs tight — so crews only have to think about dancing.

Competition

Judging & registration

International judges, the official HHI scoring system and a fair, transparent registration process.

Community

The scene

From volunteers to the floor team: the championship runs on people from the Dutch hip hop scene itself.
