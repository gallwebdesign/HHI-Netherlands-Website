Regulations

Rules of the floor.

A plain-language summary of how the championship works. The official HHI Rules & Regulations are always leading.

01

Divisions & ages

Junior (7–12), Varsity (13–17), Adult (18+), plus MiniCrew and MegaCrew open to all ages. Your division is set by your dancers’ ages on competition day.

02

Crew size

Crews are 5–9 dancers, MiniCrews exactly 3, MegaCrews 10–40. Each dancer may compete in only one crew per division.

03

Routine & music

Routine length and music requirements are set per division in the official rulebook. Music must be clean — no explicit lyrics — and submitted before the deadline.

04

Judging

An international judging panel scores by the HHI system: performance and skill, weighing musicality, creativity, execution and variety of street-dance styles.

05

Deductions

Falls, stalled routines and rulebook violations cost points. The head judge decides, and that decision is final.

06

Attire & props

Outfits are free within the rulebook’s guidelines. Loose props and set pieces are restricted — check the official document before you build.
